How Does This Tool Work? (How to Use)


Why normal spaces do not align in WhatsApp. WhatsApp normally uses a proportional font, where every letter has a different width. An "i" is narrow and an "m" is wide, so ten spaces never line up the same way on two different lines. Text wrapped in triple backticks (```) switches WhatsApp to a monospace font, where every character is exactly the same width. This tool pads each column to a fixed number of characters, so inside the backticks the table looks straight and neat.


    

  1. Step 1 – Shop and customer. Type your shop name once; it is remembered. The invoice number is editable: type any number or text such as INV-001 or 1025, or keep the suggested next number. Turn off "Show invoice number on bill" if you do not want it printed. Customer name and phone are optional, but a phone number lets you send the bill straight to that customer.
    2. 

  2. Step 2 – Items. Each row has an item name, a quantity, and a rate (price for one unit). Tap "Add Item" to add a new row. Tap the X button to remove a row; at least one row always stays. Rows with no item name are ignored.
    3. 

  3. Step 3 – Delivery and discount. Enter a delivery charge if you have one. Enter a discount and choose whether it is a flat amount or a percentage. The total is: subtotal (quantity × rate for every item) minus the discount, plus delivery. A discount can never be more than the subtotal, and a negative discount is treated as zero.
    4. 

  4. Step 4 – Live preview. The preview updates as you type. The width is fixed at 30 characters so every line fits a standard mobile screen without wrapping. Long item names continue on the next line.
    5. 

  5. Step 5 – Copy or send. Tap "Copy Text" and paste into any WhatsApp chat; the backticks are included automatically, so do not edit them. Or tap "Send on WhatsApp". If a phone number is entered, that customer's chat opens directly (a 10-digit number gets the country code added). If not, WhatsApp lets you choose a contact.
    6. 

  6. Step 6 – Print. Tap "Print Bill" to print only the receipt, in a black monospace font with no backticks. Choose 58mm, 80mm, or A4 first. To save a PDF, pick "Save as PDF" as the printer in the print dialog.
    7. 

  7. Step 7 – Download. "Download Bill" lets you save a sharp PNG image (great for WhatsApp), a plain text file, or a PDF (through the print dialog). Files are named Invoice-number-customer-date.
    8. 

  8. Step 8 – Clear / New Receipt. Clears the items, customer, discount and delivery, keeps your shop name, and moves the invoice number forward by one. You are asked to confirm first.
    9.

FAQ


Is my data saved on the internet?
No, everything stays in your own browser. There is no server.


Will this work offline?
Yes, once the page is saved or loaded, it works without internet.


Why should I avoid emojis in the bill?
Emojis are wider than normal characters and break the column alignment. This tool removes them from the receipt.


Why does the alignment look wrong in WhatsApp?
Make sure the triple backticks at the start and end of the message were not removed or edited, and that you copied the full text.
