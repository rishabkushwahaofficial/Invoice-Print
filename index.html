How Does This Tool Work? (How to Use)

 
Why does WhatsApp need triple backticks?

 
WhatsApp normally shows messages in a font where every letter has a different width, so spaces you type never line up into straight columns. When you wrap text in three backticks (```) at the start and end, WhatsApp switches that part of the message to a monospace font, where every character is exactly the same width. That is why the table in this tool looks straight and neat after you send it.

 
    
  
  1. Step 1 – Fill in the details. 
    Type your business name, phone number and optional address. If you need a tax number, turn on “Add Tax / PAN / VAT number” and a new box appears. Business details are saved in your browser and fill in automatically next time. The invoice number is created for you (like #INV-101), but you can click it and type any number or text. Pick a date, choose your currency symbol, and enter the customer’s name and mobile number. You can switch off “Show invoice number on bill” if you do not want it printed.
    2. 
  
  2. Step 2 – Add items. 
    Write a description, quantity (Qty) and Rate for each item. The Amount is worked out automatically as Qty × Rate. Press “+ Add Item” for a new row, and press the X button to remove a row. At least one row always stays. Empty rows are ignored on the bill.
    3. 
  
  3. Step 3 – Delivery and discount. 
    Enter a delivery charge if there is one. For a discount, choose “%” for a percentage or “Flat” for a fixed amount. The Grand Total is: Subtotal − Discount + Delivery. A discount can never be negative or larger than the subtotal.
    4. 
  
  4. Step 4 – Live preview. 
    The preview updates as you type and has three tabs: WhatsApp, A4 Invoice and Thermal Slip. The WhatsApp text is fixed at 30 characters wide because that fits comfortably on most phone screens without wrapping, which would break the columns.
    5. 
  
  5. Step 5 – Format A (WhatsApp). 
    Tap “Send via WhatsApp” to open WhatsApp with the bill ready to send. If the customer number has 10 digits, the country code from the box above is added. If there is no number, WhatsApp lets you pick a contact. Or tap “Copy Text” and paste it into any chat. Do not delete the backticks.
    6. 
  
  6. Step 6 – Format B (A4). 
    Tap “Print / Save as PDF”. Only the clean A4 invoice is shown in the print dialog. Choose your printer, or choose “Save as PDF” as the destination to get a PDF file.
    7. 
  
  7. Step 7 – Format C (Thermal). 
    Pick 58mm or 80mm paper width, then tap “Print Thermal Slip”. In the print dialog select your thermal printer, set the paper size to match (58mm or 80mm), set margins to none, and turn off “Headers and footers”.
    8. 
  
  8. Step 8 – Download. 
    Use the Image button for a PNG picture, Text for a .txt file, or PDF to open the print dialog and save as PDF. File names look like Invoice-INV-101-Rahul-2026-11-10.
    9. 
  
  9. Step 9 – Clear / New Invoice. 
    This empties the items, customer, discount and delivery, keeps your business details, and moves the invoice number forward (INV-101 becomes INV-102). You will be asked to confirm first.

FAQ

No, everything stays in your own browser. There is no server and no login.

 
Will this work offline?
Yes, once the page is loaded or saved, it works without internet.

 
Why should I avoid emojis in the bill?
Emojis are wider than normal characters and break column alignment.

 
Why does the alignment look wrong in WhatsApp?
